# Portfolio study

Collected 6 Oct 2026 for the static site in this folder. The public page does not list these sites.

## What the galleries reward

Sources: [perfolios](https://perfolios.shwn.design/) (215 hand-picked personal sites; 128 of them were on the page fetched here), [Awwwards portfolio honors](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/winner_category_portfolio/), [Awwwards personal nominees](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/personal/), [Godly](https://godly.website/) (now also reached as Recent), and the 2026 gallery roundups on Colorlib and Muzli.

Three patterns show up on the sites that still feel current:

- **Motion.** A moving line of type, a slow field, and a scroll cue. The sites that stay readable do not hide the cursor or lock the page to a smooth-scroll library.
- **Swap.** A list on one side and a preview that changes with the row. Awwwards calls this out on Portfolio ’26 as a list/grid interaction. Portraits and roles swap the same way.
- **Art.** Original marks, crop frames, and a palette tied to the photography. The engineer portfolios that work do not look like a SaaS template.

Those three are what `motion.js` and the art plates implement. No site below was copied.
